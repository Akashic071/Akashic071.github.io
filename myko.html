MyKo.

TU/e · M11 Project 2025 Group Project Children Aged 2–4

About MyKo.

MyKo is a bio-based toy-making toolkit designed for children aged 2–4. By combining natural materials, mycelium growth, and open-ended play, the project invites children to become active creators of their own toys. From collecting materials outdoors to shaping and observing biological growth, MyKo explores how hands-on making can encourage sensory engagement, curiosity, and awareness of natural processes.
