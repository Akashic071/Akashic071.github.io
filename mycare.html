MyCare.

A Caring and Trustworthy Friend for Women's Health

Undergraduate Project SUSTech Concept Design Product & UX/UI

About MyCare.

MyCare is a conceptual at-home HPV screening system designed to explore a more private and accessible approach to women's health. Combining a reusable menstrual pad, a portable detection kit, and a companion mobile app, the project proposes an integrated experience from sample collection to result viewing, with an emphasis on usability, privacy, and personal health awareness.
