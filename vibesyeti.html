VibesYeti.

From Absence to Embrace — Exploring Parent–Child Connection Through Tactile Interaction

SUSTech Undergraduate Graduation Project 2025 Individual Project

About VibesYeti.

VibesYeti is an interactive plush toy designed to explore emotional connections between children and their parents during periods of separation. Through gentle low-frequency vibrations, it translates familiar sounds such as heartbeats, breathing, humming, and singing into tactile experiences. Combining soft textiles with embedded electronics, the project investigates how touch can communicate a sense of presence beyond physical distance.
